We’re Emily and Craig, a husband-and-wife team who make learning feel like play. Emily leads the learning design and builds; Craig brings the gamer’s eye for hidden details.

Emily Chorley

Learning designer and eLearning developer

Emily has been designing and building digital learning since 2010, and won two Platinum awards at the LearnX LIVE! Awards 2020 for interactive scenario and game design.

She works end to end, from scripting to the finished SCORM package, and specialises in Articulate Storyline: gamified scenarios, simulations, mini-games and, more recently, AI-powered learning tools. Her work includes courses for the WHO Academy, EA Games, Porsche and Nissan, often in regulated, high-stakes settings.

With a degree in Graphic Communication with Typography, she designs the look of a course as carefully as the learning in it.

Craig Chorley

Game elements and detail

Craig is the newer half of Team Chorley and our resident gamer. He brings close attention to detail to every build.

He specialises in hidden game elements: the Easter eggs, secrets and small surprises that reward curious learners and keep them exploring from start to finish.
