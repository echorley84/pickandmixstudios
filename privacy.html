Privacy notice

Your data, your rules

Last updated: 12 November 2026

We only collect what you choose to send us. We don’t track you, and we never sell your details.

Who we are

Pick and Mix Studios, based in Portugal, is responsible for your information. Contact us at hello (at) pickandmixstudios.com.

What we collect

  • Messages: if you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address, message and anything else you choose to add. We use it only to reply to you, because you asked us to. The form is delivered to our inbox by a service called Web3Forms.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe to Tiny Bytes, your email address is held by beehiiv, which sends it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Server logs: like every website, our web host keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address and pages visited) to keep the site secure and running.

What we don’t do

No tracking or advertising cookies, no analytics that follow you, and no selling or sharing your details for marketing. Your reading settings (theme, text size and so on) are saved only in your own browser and never sent to us.

Other services

Videos play from YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode) only when you press play. The translate button uses Google Translate only when you click it. Our demos are hosted by Articulate. When you use these, or follow links to sites like Gumroad, Patreon or social media, their own privacy policies apply.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and for our normal business records.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, delete or stop using your information, or for a copy of it. Just email us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal that’s the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).
