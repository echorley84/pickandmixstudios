Privacy notice

Your data, your rules

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This notice explains what information we collect when you use pickandmixstudios.com, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we only collect what you choose to send us, we don’t track you, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Pick and Mix Studios is run by Emily and Craig Chorley, based in Portugal. We’re responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us at hello@pickandmixstudios.com.

What we collect and why

When you send us a message

Our contact form asks for your name, email address and message, plus what it’s about and, if you choose, your organisation. We use these only to reply to you and, if you ask, to share our client work with you. We don’t add you to any mailing list.

The form is delivered by Web3Forms (opens in a new tab), which passes your message on to our email inbox. If you email us directly, we receive the same kind of information.

We use this information because you asked us to get in touch (and, where it applies, to take steps towards working together).

If you subscribe to Tiny Bytes

Our free newsletter is sent by beehiiv (opens in a new tab), which stores your email address for us. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • We don’t use analytics that follow you around the web.
  • We don’t sell or share your details for marketing.

Settings saved on your device

If you change the reading settings (theme, text size, font, spacing, animation, simple page), your choices are saved in your own browser so they’re remembered next time, including on our Tiny Bytes pages. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. Use Reset all in the Aa panel to clear them.

Other services the site uses

  • Hosting: our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.
  • Videos: our tutorial and showcase videos play from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode, and only load when you press play. YouTube’s own privacy policy applies once a video is playing.
  • Translation: the globe button opens a translated copy of the page through Google Translate. Nothing is sent to Google unless you use it.
  • Demos and shops: our playable demos are hosted by Articulate, and links to Gumroad, Patreon, LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook take you to their sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Our fonts are hosted on this site, so loading a page doesn’t contact any font service.

How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and for our normal business records, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can also ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Just email hello@pickandmixstudios.com and we’ll help.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD (opens in a new tab), or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
